Gullwing doors + cantilever rack

A VA canopy where the whole side lifts up like a wing and the roof rack runs forward over the cab. Built on the Hilux GD6 DC master (TOD6 v1.2 / 40xxx data pack), 1.5 mm 3CR12, welded and powder coated as now.

Base: Hilux GD6 DC masterMaterial: 3CR12 1.5 mmStatus: concept, not yet drawn in CAD

4 · Engineering changes to the GD6 DC master

Side doors (both sides)

  • Delete 40106 / 40099 (Bo Links / Regs Bo) as separate panels. The roof flange and a 40 mm return on the corner posts form the top of the aperture.
  • Shrink 40110 / 40101 (Onder) to an 85 mm sill strip with the seal land and two lock keeps.
  • New door replaces 40109 / 40102: 1 450 × 400 outer, 1.5 mm 3CR12, 20 mm hem all round as now, plus one 25 mm Z-stiffener welded along the inside at mid-height. A 1.5 mm skin this size oil-cans without it.
  • Hinge: 304 stainless piano hinge, 1 450 long, 1.5 mm leaf, riveted or plug-welded to the roof flange and door hem. Roof drip flange overhangs it by 10 mm.
  • Struts: 2 per door. Door mass estimate 9 kg steel + 3 kg glass, centre of mass 200 mm below hinge, closed moment ≈ 24 N·m. Size with Struts Africa (current part SA4041) for hold-open at 85° and a controlled last 150 mm of close. Brackets GB-1003/4/5 move to the corner posts.
  • Locks: one Furnlock T-handle driving a 2-point rod latch, or two push-button locks 1 100 mm apart. One lock on a 1.45 m door leaves the far end rattling.
  • Seal: Rubberite bulb seal on the aperture flange all round, same as the rear door. Add two drain slots in the sill strip.
  • Window: Alwin glass in the door as now, 1 100 × 250 cut-out, or a blank "N" version.

Cantilever roof rack

  • Extend RRD-1001 / 1002 side bodies from ~1 500 to 2 330 mm. Keep the 57 mm folded C profile over the canopy; on the 700 mm cantilever double it to a closed 57 × 30 box by welding a second folded C inside, or go to 2 mm for that part.
  • 7 × RRD-1003 cross bars (was 5), pitch ≈ 320 mm.
  • 6 × RRD-1004 feet (was 4). The new front pair bolt through the roof into the TOD6-11 corner-post gussets, not into open roof skin.
  • Add one 3 mm gusset plate (like 40165-series brackets) inside each front corner where the post meets the roof, to take the cantilever moment into the body.
  • First-pass numbers: 100 kg spread over the rack puts ≈ 30 kg on the overhang, ≈ 55 N·m per side rail at the front edge, ×3 for corrugations ≈ 160 N·m. The 57 mm C section carries that at under 50 MPa. The weak spot is the foot-to-roof joint, not the rail. Prove by load test before publishing a rating.
  • Rating to publish after test: target 150 kg dynamic / 300 kg static. Storm's call.
  • Cab clearance: the rack must clear the cab roof by 50 mm with the canopy on its rubbers under load. Hilux antenna and any factory roof rails to be checked per vehicle.

Not changing

Footprints (TOD6-01/02), front panel (TOD6-03), rear door (TOD6-04 / 40104), roof (40105) except the hinge flange, corners, hold-down brackets (40165–40171), material, powder coat, colours.

Utemaster charges roughly NZD 1 900 (≈ R20 000) for the rack alone. Even at double the cost delta above, a VA "Wing + Long Rack" upgrade could sit well under R6 000 and still carry margin. Price is Storm's decision; nothing here is a price.

6 · Build plan

  1. Measure on a GD6 DC with canopy fitted: rail-to-cab-roof height, antenna position, distance from canopy front edge to windscreen top. Record in the fitment library.
  2. Prototype one wing door on the left side of the next GD6 build. Keep the right side standard so the two can be compared on the same bakkie.
  3. Send Struts Africa the door mass, hinge-to-strut geometry and 85° target; fit their recommended pair.
  4. Build the long rack as a bolt-on to the existing roof, test with 150 kg static on the overhang and a corrugated-road drive with 75 kg before rating it.
  5. If both pass, update the TOD6 drawings to v1.3 and the BOM, then re-shoot the brochure gallery with the wings up. That photo sells it.

7 · Notes

  • Top-hinged full-side doors and over-cab racks are generic features. RSI SmartCap already sells a gullwing steel canopy in SA. Don't use the names Centurion, Cantilever Roof Rack or Bandit Proof in VA marketing; call them what VA calls them.
  • VA's edge over the aluminium canopy stays the same: welded 1.5 mm steel, no rivets to shear, powder coat, and roughly a third of the landed price.
