01 · Brand Overview
WHO WE ARE

Veld Armour is a proudly South African steel canopy manufacturer based in Florida, Roodepoort, Gauteng. We build premium CNC laser-cut steel canopies for South African bakkies — purpose-built for each vehicle make and model, hand-welded by skilled craftspeople, and finished to a standard the veld demands.

We were founded in 2026 with a simple belief: South African bakkie owners deserve a quality, locally-made steel canopy at a fair price, delivered on time, every time.

Tagline: Built for the Veld
Mission: Build the best steel canopy in South Africa and deliver it in 7 working days.
Vision: The most trusted canopy brand for South African bakkie owners.

04 · Logo & Shield
BRAND MARK

The Veld Armour brand mark consists of two elements: the VA Shield and the VELD ARMOUR wordmark. Always use them together. Never alter the proportions, colours or letterforms.

VELD ARMOUR
Primary — Dark background
VELD ARMOUR
Reversed — Light background

Clear space: Always maintain a minimum clear space equal to the height of the "V" in VELD around the entire brand mark. Never place the logo on a busy background without a solid colour backing.
